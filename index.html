World view

The world view displays a global overview of emissions at a set time. The area of a circle is proportional to the total GHG emissions of the country.

Circles are coloured according to the percentage change of total emissions over a set period of time. This is done on a log scale.

The overlap correction checkbox toggles a force simulation on the circles. This distorts the geography, but allows for inspection of countries that may be otherwise obscured.

Timeline

Move the circular handle to change selected year. Move the bar shaped handle to change the year in which the percentage change calculations start from.

Country view

The country view displays a detailed breakdown of a country's emissions over time. Hover over the areas in order to see which colours correspond to which sectors. You are able to filter the gases or switch from absolute to relative measurements.
